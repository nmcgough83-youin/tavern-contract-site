Support

The Tavern Contract is a pass-and-play game of contracts, secret roles and quiet betrayal for 2–6 players sharing one phone, playable over 3, 4, or 5 rounds.

How to play

Full rules are shown in the "How to Play" button inside the app, and are also available as a printable PDF here.

Having a problem?

If something isn't working as expected, email taverncontract@gmail.com with your device model and iOS version and a description of what happened.

Feedback

Suggestions for new roles, contracts, or features are welcome at the same address above.
