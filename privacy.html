Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

The Tavern Contract ("the app") is a local pass-and-play party game. This policy explains what data the app does — and does not — collect.

Data we collect

None. The Tavern Contract does not require an account, does not connect to any server while you play, and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, usage data, or analytics. Everything that happens in a game — player count, role assignments, scores — stays on the device and is cleared when you close or reset the game.

In-app purchase

The app offers one optional one-time purchase (a "Bonus Pack" of extra roles and contracts). That transaction is handled entirely by Apple's App Store — we never see or store your payment information, and purchase records live in your Apple ID, not with us.

Third parties

The app does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or any other third-party tracking. It makes no network requests during normal play.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it does not collect data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds features that collect data (for example, optional analytics), this page will be updated first and the change will be reflected in the app's App Store listing.
